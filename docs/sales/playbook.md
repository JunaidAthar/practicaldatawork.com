# Sales playbook (short)

**One-liner:** We make sure you get every Medicare negotiated-price refund you're owed, on time, and you only pay if we recover money.

**Targets first:** independents with no 340B contracts (verify in OPAIS) · owner-pharmacists with 1–5 stores and a high Medicare share · long-term care pharmacies · multi-store owners.

**Channels:** NCPA Annual Convention (Kansas City, Oct 3–6, 2026) · local walk-ins Tue–Thu 1:30–3:30pm · Illinois Pharmacists Association webinar · co-ops (IPC, Elevate, Health Mart Atlas, Leader) after 5–10 audits · pharmacy-specialist CPA firms · pharmacy brokers/consultants · owner Facebook groups · NCPDP dataQ lists filtered against OPAIS.

**Opener:** "How many days are your Eliquis and Jardiance refunds running? Most owners I talk to are at 3–4 weeks, and a chunk never show up."

**The ask:** Free audit. Three things after a BAA: Beacon user, MTF vendor designation, one dispensing export.

**Objections:** co-op already helps → keep them, we do claim-level chasing · no time → 30 min then we do it · data/HIPAA → BAA first, read-only, minimum necessary · who are you → free audit, judge the number · 25% too much → 25% of money you're not getting; tier down for 3+ stores · "send info" → book 15 minutes Thursday.
