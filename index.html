FAQ

Questions owners ask

My co-op already helps with this. Why would I need you?

Keep them. Co-ops help with enrollment and flag problems. We go claim by claim and do the chasing with each manufacturer and CMS. The free audit shows whether anything is slipping through.

What exactly do you need from me?

After we sign a HIPAA agreement: add us as a Beacon user, name us as your third-party vendor on the Medicare Transaction Facilitator (MTF), and send one dispensing export for the negotiated drugs. About 30 minutes.

What if I have 340B contracts?

We can still help. We'll also use reports from your 340B administrator to compare their determinations with the manufacturers' flags. If you have no 340B contracts, we verify that against HRSA's public 340B registry to overturn wrongful denials.

How much does it cost?

The audit is free. Recovery is 25% of what we actually recover, with nothing upfront. Monitoring is $199 a month, month to month.

Which drugs are covered?

All Part D drugs in the Medicare Drug Price Negotiation Program: 10 drugs in 2026, with 15 more (including Ozempic and Wegovy) starting January 2027.

Do you contact my patients, PBM or wholesaler?

No. We work from refund payment files, Beacon reports and your records. Disputes go to manufacturers and, when needed, CMS. We may ask for wholesaler invoices to support an underpayment.
