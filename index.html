The problem

Since January, every negotiated drug you fill leaves you waiting on a check.

Medicare now pays a negotiated price for drugs like Eliquis, Jardiance, Xarelto, Farxiga, Januvia and Entresto. You still buy them near list price, so the manufacturer owes you the difference as a refund, often hundreds of dollars per fill. Those refunds are slow, hard to reconcile, and sometimes never arrive.

67%of independent pharmacies wait 22+ days for manufacturer refunds.
78%have trouble reconciling refunds to their claims.
15 moredrugs join in January 2027, including Ozempic and Wegovy.
Refunds are also being denied as "340B" (code N907) at pharmacies that have no 340B contracts. Those denials can be overturned.

Sources: NCPA survey of independent pharmacies, February 2026; KFF; NCPA, August 2026.

Security & HIPAA

Your data is handled like it matters, because it does.

We act as your HIPAA business associate. Nothing moves until the agreement is signed.

  • BAA first. We sign a HIPAA business associate agreement before we see any claim data.
  • Read-only. We read payment files and reports. We never touch your dispensing system.
  • Minimum necessary. We only request negotiated-drug claims, not your full dispensing history.
  • No email attachments. Files move through the MTF and Beacon portals or an encrypted upload link. Never email patient information, including through the form below.
  • Encrypted and logged. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is logged, and data is deleted on a set schedule.

FAQ

Questions owners ask

My co-op already helps with this. Why would I need you?

Keep them. Co-ops help with enrollment and flag problems. We go claim by claim and do the chasing: filing disputes with each manufacturer and escalating through CMS. The free audit shows whether anything is slipping through. If it's clean, you've lost nothing.

What exactly do you need from me?

Three things, after we sign a HIPAA agreement: add us as a user on your Beacon account, name us as your third-party vendor on the Medicare Transaction Facilitator (MTF), and send one dispensing export for the negotiated drugs. It takes about 30 minutes.

What if I have 340B contracts?

We can still help. We'll also need reports from your 340B administrator so we can compare their 340B determinations with the manufacturers' flags. If you have no 340B contracts, we verify that against HRSA's public 340B registry and use it to overturn wrongful denials.

How much does it cost?

The audit is free. Recovery is 25% of what we actually recover, with nothing upfront. Ongoing monitoring is $199 a month, month to month.

Which drugs are covered?

All Part D drugs in the Medicare Drug Price Negotiation Program. That's 10 drugs in 2026, with 15 more (including Ozempic and Wegovy) starting in January 2027.

Do you contact my patients, my PBM or my wholesaler?

No. We work with the refund payment files, Beacon reports and your records. Disputes go to the manufacturers and, when needed, CMS. We may ask you for wholesaler invoices to support an underpayment claim.
